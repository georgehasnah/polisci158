Campaign Trail: Donor Edition

A tiny campaign game with a Q-learning agent. Built for POLISCI 158 (AI & Politics), Section 6. You're a candidate at headquarters (H). Reach the Capitol (C) within 30 moves. A donor office (D) gives money once per game. A rally stage (R) can be used again and again, but needs 3 moves to reset. Scandals (S) end the campaign. Dark squares are closed roads.
